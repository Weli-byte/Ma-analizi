# ADR 0088 — Phase 2, Sprint 10: over/under 2.5 and both teams to score (2026-10-29)

Plan: `Futbol_Tahmin_Optimizasyonu_Sprint_7_12_ULTRA_PRO_MAX.pdf`, sprint 10. Result: **CONDITIONAL** — the label contract, the probability consistency of every published forecast and the live comparison with trivial baselines are audited on real data; the candidate models the plan asks for were already tested on the development folds by the league programme (Sprints 3, 4, 4c, 4d), the one missing family (bivariate Poisson / negative binomial) was not added; **no served number changes** and no model is approved. Nothing was bought, no data was invented.

## What was found
1. **Labels are the 90-minute score.** Read from the code, not assumed: football-data.org `regularTime` beyond 90 minutes, openfootball cup lines use the 90-minute figure (an extra-time result without it is unusable), odds-provider and ESPN-backfill scores are accepted only in the league phase / as 90-minute scores, awarded and abandoned matches are not in the record.
2. **The published forecasts are internally consistent.** 7,658 stored artifacts (all of the single model version `markets-1.0.0`, 6,771 with the total-goals distribution): 0 violations of the invariants (probabilities in [0, 1], 1X2 sums to 1, over + under = 1 on every line, BTTS yes + no = 1, double chance / draw-no-bet follow from the headline, over lines fall with the line, scorelines sorted and at most 1 in sum, the total-goals distribution sums to 1 and gives the stored over 2.5). A corrupted artifact is flagged, never repaired (test).
3. **"Always over" is a feature of this sample, not a stable benchmark.** The live record (202 settled matches) is 64.9 % over 2.5 (95 % interval 58.0–71.1 %), the pooled rate of the 10,712 development matches is 53.5 %; BTTS is 60.4 % live against 54.3 %. The production over/under forecast (mean 55.3 %) is shifted low against this sample (observed 64.9 %), which is why it trails "always over" in accuracy (62.9 % vs 64.9 %). Against the historical constant prevalence its log loss is better by -0.0218, but with 4 weekly blocks the interval is not usable (`TOO_FEW_WEEKLY_BLOCKS`).
4. **On the development folds the production model is worse than a constant prevalence** on both goal markets (log loss +0.0050 for over/under, +0.0072 for BTTS against the training rate, stored means of `s4c_metrics.json`). The candidates that beat it with a paired interval wholly below 0 (Sprint 4c: `H_dc_hier` -0.0122 over/under, -0.0072 BTTS; `G_base` -0.0112 / -0.0088 against `production_global`) gain only 0.006–0.009 log loss over the prevalence on over/under and **nothing measurable on BTTS** (`H_dc_hier` -0.0000, `G_base` -0.0016). **BTTS carries almost no usable skill beyond the base rate with the data we have.**
5. **Experimental card (ADR 0072/0073), 48 live rows:** over/under 0.6743 vs production 0.6546 (+0.0197), BTTS 0.6963 vs 0.6843 (+0.0119); both inside their intervals and both with only two weekly blocks: no evidence either way, the card stays BETA under its own pre-registered gate.

## Decisions
- No change to any forecast, card, gate or flag. The experimental model is not promoted; the production model is kept (the plan: "if the model does not improve, keep production").
- Candidates not added: **bivariate Poisson and negative binomial**, **bookmaker-implied over/under** (`BLOCKED`: only 1X2 quotes are collected) and a new direct classifier. They would need a row-level re-run of the nested walk-forward harness on the CI runner and development folds only (the frozen holdout of the league programme is consumed and is never read). Pre-registered for such a run: report against the training-rate baseline with a paired block interval, per-league slices only with the sample sizes of Sprint 4c, and no global rollout from league-level gains.
- `python -m src.goalmkts.report --markets-root <markets-data checkout> --out docs/goalmkts/sprint10_goal_markets.md` reproduces the document; the paired verdict is `TOO_FEW_WEEKLY_BLOCKS` below 8 weekly blocks.
