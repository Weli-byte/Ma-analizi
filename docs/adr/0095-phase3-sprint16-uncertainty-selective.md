# ADR 0095 — Phase 3, Sprint 16: uncertainty, selective prediction and confidence labels (2026-11-01)

Plan: `Futbol_Tahmin_Platformu_Faz3_Sprint_13_18_ULTRA_PRO_MAX.pdf`, sprint 16. Release decision: **CONDITIONAL_GO** — the selective layer is validated on an untouched season and on the live record and is served; the least-confident bucket has too few live matches for a confidence claim (INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE), and the live record is one early season.

## Existing pieces (audited)
Probabilities come from the market model (and, for the 1X2 headline, the de-vigged market, ADR 0041); the identity is the champion calibrator (ADR 0089); the user labels `strong lean` / `lean` / `toss-up` were checked prospectively in ADR 0089. There was no abstain rule, no coverage-risk curve and no per-forecast uncertainty field.

## Decision
- Measures, each under its own name (`src/uncertainty/measures.py`): the model's top 1X2 probability, entropy, top-1 minus top-2 margin, half-L1 disagreement between model and market, data flags (no exact odds, a club with little history). No single invented confidence score.
- Coverage levels 100 / 80 / 60 / 40 % fixed in advance; the threshold of a level is the quantile of the model's top probability on the out-of-fold predictions of 2020-21 to 2022-23 (5,898 matches); scored once on the untouched 2023-24 season and on the live record (`src/uncertainty/selective.py`, `study.py`). Abstained matches stay in the coverage denominator; the selected accuracy is always shown next to the all-matches one.
- Served (`src/uncertainty/serving.py`): the intelligence response carries `uncertainty` — `calibrated_probability`, `calibration`, entropy, margin, disagreement, `coverage_bucket` (40 / 60 / 80 / abstain), `abstain`, `abstain_reason`, `uncertainty_reason`, the bucket's live evidence (INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE under 50 settled matches) and a "not a guarantee, not betting advice" note. The match page shows a "Model güveni" card. **An abstaining forecast shows no value suggestion** (the abstain decision wins over an expected-value signal; nothing is ever placed as a bet).
- Thresholds and evidence live in `docs/phase3/s16_selective.json`, regenerated by `python -m src.uncertainty.study`, shipped in the site bundle.

## Evidence (`docs/phase3/sprint16_report.md`)
- Untouched season 2023-24 (1,752 matches, model only): accuracy 53.0 % at full coverage, 56.2 % at 80 %, 60.5 % at 60 %, 66.4 % at 40 % (log loss 0.976 → 0.838); realised coverages 100 / 81.5 / 60.9 / 39.8 % — the thresholds transfer.
- Live record (237 settled matches): 62.0 / 66.8 / 70.8 / 80.0 % at 100 / 80 / 60 / 40 % coverage. Buckets: top 40 % 85 matches, stated 66.2 %, hit 80.0 % (70.3–87.1); the abstain bucket 41 matches, hit 39.0 % — INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE. The top bucket understates its hit rate on this early-season sample; no "high confidence" promise is made from it.
- Data flags (live): with exact odds 62.7 % (209), model only 57.1 % (28). Exact score: top-1 8.9 %, top-3 30.8 %, top-5 43.9 % (237).
- Tests: `tests/test_uncertainty.py` (measures, history thresholds, denominators, study on real excerpts, served block, abstain on a TEST_ONLY near coin-flip, API, page, abstain hides value suggestions, bundle).

## Not done
Epistemic / aleatoric split beyond the data flags (no ensemble or posterior to measure model uncertainty); an out-of-distribution score beyond the low-history flag; per-league thresholds (too few matches per league).
