# ADR 0096 — Phase 3, Sprint 17: ablation laboratory and promotion gates (2026-11-01)

Plan: `Futbol_Tahmin_Platformu_Faz3_Sprint_13_18_ULTRA_PRO_MAX.pdf`, sprint 17. Release decision: **CONDITIONAL_GO** — the laboratory, the leakage gate and the promotion gate work and every experiment the free data allows was run once; no candidate passed, the champion stays, and four experiments are NOT_RUN on their data or budget gates.

## Decision
- **Pre-registration** (`src/experiments/registry.py`): an experiment is a spec (hypothesis, track, market, candidate, baselines, primary and secondary metrics, minimal practical effect 0.002 log loss, window, stage, decision rule, data requirements). Its canonical-JSON SHA-256 is committed in `docs/phase3/experiments/<id>/spec.json` BEFORE any result exists (commit of the specs precedes the run). A result is refused when the spec's hash changed or the experiment already has a result; a changed protocol is a new id. Re-running to pick a better number is impossible by construction.
- **Champion frozen** in `docs/phase3/experiments/champion.json`: `markets-1.0.0`, the markets config hash, identity calibrator (ADR 0089), the de-vigged market as 1X2 headline when exact odds exist (ADR 0087).
- **Leakage gate** (`src/experiments/gates.py`): out-of-fold rows carry the fit time of their model (`fit_as_of`, new optional field of `calibsel.oof.build_oof(with_rates=True)`); a row predicted by a model fitted after its kick-off, a final-test season, or overlapping fit and test seasons fails the gate. Live: odds observed after the forecast cutoff fail the gate; post-kick-off forecasts are counted and never scored.
- **Promotion gate**: the Bonferroni interval of candidate minus champion wholly below -0.002, no league with at least 100 matches whose interval is wholly above 0, ECE at most 0.005 worse, coverage at least 95 %, no paid call, leakage gate passed, result reproduced. A pass makes a PROMOTION_CANDIDATE (a recommendation; promotion itself stays an owner decision with shadow / canary, ADR 0068 / 0076).
- **Matrix** (`src/experiments/lab.py`, run once by `.github/workflows/experiments-lab.yml` because the out-of-fold rebuild does not fit the owner's PC memory): champion vs naive baselines on the untouched 2023-24 season and on the live record; calibration, odds and league-model families read from their stored, already-gated evidence; bivariate Poisson and negative binomial score grids (the open goal-family study of ADR 0091); xG, line-ups, LLM and trained stacking recorded as NOT_RUN with their gates.

## Evidence (`docs/phase3/sprint17_report.md`, results in `docs/phase3/experiments/*/result.json`)
- Out-of-fold rows rebuilt in the cloud: 7,650 rows, 43 fits, 16,663 history matches, `dv-of-56d45e54d516` — the same data version, fit count and history as ADR 0089; all 1,000 rows of the committed excerpt agree to within 4.4e-13 (the byte digest differs by operating-system float noise only). Leakage gate PASS.
- E01, 2023-24 (1,752 matches, model only) vs the 2020-23 base rates: 1X2 log loss 0.976 vs 1.077 (delta -0.101, interval -0.120 / -0.081), over 2.5 0.668 vs 0.690 (-0.022, -0.031 / -0.013), BTTS 0.681 vs 0.687 (-0.006, -0.013 / +0.001: **not demonstrated**). Accuracy 53.0 % vs always-home 43.1 %.
- E02, live record (259 matches, 4 weekly blocks: TOO_FEW_WEEKLY_BLOCKS): 1X2 0.895 vs 1.038; over 2.5 0.659 vs 0.682; **BTTS 0.691 vs 0.684, worse than the base rate** (+0.007, +0.003 / +0.012). Accuracy 62.9 % vs always-home 50.2 %. The BTTS shortfall is a finding to watch; with four weeks it is not grounds for a methodological change.
- E03 calibration, E04 odds, E05 league models: NO_GAIN on their stored evidence (identity kept on every target; market weight 1.0; 0 league models approved on the one-shot holdout).
- E06 bivariate Poisson (theta 0.02) and E07 negative binomial (r 160, i.e. close to Poisson) vs the champion's Dixon-Coles grid: no market improves; 1X2 is slightly worse (+0.0011 and +0.0017). Shapes reproduced on a second fit. NO_GAIN; the goal-family question of ADR 0091 is closed for now.
- E08 xG, E09 line-ups, E11 stacking: NOT_RUN (data gates of ADR 0085 / 0086 / 0087 not met). E10 LLM: NOT_RUN (real calls cost money; the owner's budget is 0; no mock enters).
- Tests: `tests/test_experiments.py` (committed specs match their hashes, registration before result, one result only, changed spec refused, leakage gate on TEST_ONLY late fits / locked seasons / overlaps and live odds after the cutoff, every promotion check, the matrix on real excerpts, goal families through the gate on the real `real_oof_rates_excerpt.jsonl`, CLI).

## Not done
Per-stage (t-24h / t-90m / t-30m) ablations wait for more weekly blocks of stage records (ADR 0093). A feature-group ablation of the champion itself (form, Elo prior, home advantage) would need a refactor of the market model's fit into switchable parts; out of scope.
